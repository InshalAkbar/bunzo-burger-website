SMASH BURGERS
FROM BUNZO

BUNZO started in 2023 as a weekend pop-up behind a Williamsburg bar — one flat top, one burger, five around the block. A year later we opened our first spot on Ember Street, and the idea stayed the same: short menu, done properly, served fast.

MENU

Every burger starts as a ball of fresh 90/10 chuck, smashed hard on a 400°F griddle with the edges lace-thin crisp, then American cheese, pickles, our house sauce and a potato bun baked every morning. No flavor boosters, no shortcuts.

On the side: crispy chicken, loaded fries, thick shakes and soft serve. Four burgers, a few sides and drinks — everything you want, nothing you don't.

"We wanted a burger you could eat every day and still get excited about," says our head chef.

INTERIOR

Red booths, black and white tiles and a neon sign that hums a little. BUNZO is built to feel like the diner you grew up in — loud music, quick service and an open counter where you can watch the patties hit the steel.

THE CREW

Our cooks, cashiers and dishwashers are the reason people come back. We pay above the neighborhood average, share the tips with the whole team and promote from the line first. Good burgers come from people who like their job — and it shows in every bite.
